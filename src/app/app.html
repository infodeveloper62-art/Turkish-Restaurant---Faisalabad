Bringing Ottoman Royal Kitchens to the Heart of Faisalabad

Located conveniently on Lahore - Sheikhupura - Faisalabad Road, opposite Imtiaz Mart and right beside Misaq ul Mall, TURKISH RESTAURANT was born out of an unyielding passion to bring the true soul of Anatolian gastronomy to Pakistan’s industrial capital.

Unlike standard commercial grills, our kitchen preserves ancient Ottoman techniques: every single skewer of our renowned Adana and Urfa kebab is hand-kneaded with a traditional heavy Zırh knife to retain all natural juices, seasoned solely with sweet Capsicum pepper, sumac, and sea salt, then slowly broiled over pure wood charcoal.

From our 400°C stone deck hearth producing bubbling cheese-laden Pide and wafer-thin Lahmacun, to forty-layer pistachio Baklava flown with Gaziantep butter, every dish is an invitation to taste the majestic history of Turkey.
